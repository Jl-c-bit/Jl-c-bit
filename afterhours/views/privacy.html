The short version

  • No accounts, no email, no photos, no tracking cookies, no ads, no analytics.
  • Your profile lives only in the server's memory while you're online. It's deleted when you leave.
  • Chats are not saved. The exception: if someone reports a chat, its last 20 messages are kept so a moderator can review them.
  • We never store your IP address. For bans, we keep a scrambled code that can't be turned back into it.
  • We never sell or share your data, except with service providers who run the site and with authorities when the law requires it or someone's safety is at risk.

2. Sensitive information

Who you're interested in and what you're looking for can reveal your sexual orientation or sexual practices. The Australian Privacy Act treats this as sensitive information, as do other laws such as the GDPR. We only use it with your explicit consent, which you give when you tick the consent box before going online. We use it only to match you with people, and we keep it only while you're connected (or within a report, as above). You can withdraw consent at any time by leaving the site.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other users whose preferences match yours can see your nickname, age, gender, what you're looking for, your general area, and your bio. They never see your date of birth, who you're interested in, or your network address.
  • The person you chat with sees your messages. Remember they could screenshot them. That breaks our rules, but we can't technically prevent it.
  • Moderators can see everyone online and their profiles, plus reports and their chat excerpts. They never see your IP address, only the first 8 characters of its hash. Moderators can't read chats that haven't been reported.
  • Service providers who run the site for us: our hosting and database provider, located in {{HOSTING_REGION}}. If we add an age-verification provider, it will process the data needed to check your age under its own privacy policy, and we'll list it here.
  • Authorities. We report suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation to the Australian Federal Police through the Australian Centre to Counter Child Exploitation (ACCCE), or to the equivalent authority overseas. We may share relevant information with police or the eSafety Commissioner when the law requires it, or when we believe in good faith that it's needed to prevent a serious threat to someone's life, health, or safety.

We never sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

4. Australian Privacy Principles

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). In particular:

  • We collect only what we need to run the Service safely (APP 3). We collect sensitive information only with your consent.
  • You can use the Service under a nickname. We never ask for your real name (APP 2).
  • We use information only for the purposes described here, or as the law requires or permits (APP 6).
  • When data is stored overseas, as described in section 9, we take reasonable steps to make sure the provider protects it consistently with the APPs (APP 8).
  • We delete or de-identify information when we no longer need it (APP 11).

If you're in the EU or UK

Our legal bases for processing are:

  • Contract: to provide the Service you asked to use (profile, matching, delivering messages).
  • Explicit consent: for preferences that may reveal sexual orientation or sex life (section 2).
  • Legitimate interests: keeping users safe, preventing abuse, enforcing bans, and handling reports. We think these interests are not overridden by yours, because we keep the data minimal and short-lived.
  • Legal obligation: age checks where the law requires them, and reporting illegal content.

5. Cookies and tracking

We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or browser storage for visitors. Moderators' browsers keep their sign-in for the current tab only (session storage), which is cleared when the tab closes.

6. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Moderator access is password-protected and rate-limited. Network addresses are hashed with a secret key before anything is stored, and stored data is limited to what's listed above. No system is perfectly secure. If we have a data breach that puts you at risk, we'll tell the relevant authorities and, where we can, you.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access or correct personal information we hold about you (APPs 12 and 13). Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it elsewhere. Because we don't have accounts and delete profiles when you leave, there is usually nothing left to access or delete. If you were reported or banned, or reported someone, contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the date and nickname used, and we'll help as far as we can identify the records. We may keep information about reports involving the safety of minors where the law requires it.

Complaints. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, contact us first. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're still not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au, 1300 363 992). In the UK, you can complain to the ICO, and in the EU, to your national data-protection authority.

8. Children

Afterhours is strictly for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Anyone who appears to be under 18 is removed and blocked, and we delete their data except where we must keep it to protect children or meet a legal duty. If you believe a minor is using the Service, report them in the app or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
