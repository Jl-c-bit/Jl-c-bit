# Mandala series: Amazon KDP listings (copy and paste)

All four books share these settings:

- **Format:** Paperback · 8.5 x 11 in · Black & white interior · White paper · No bleed · Matte cover
- **Files:** `out/<book>/interior.pdf` is the manuscript. Upload `out/<book>/cover.pdf` with "Upload a cover you already have".
- **Author / brand:** Jl Studio
- **Series:** after the first book is live, open KDP's **Series** page and create the series "Jl Studio Mandala Collection". Add all four books to it. Amazon then shows a series page and cross-links the books, so each one sells the others.
- **Low-content book:** No. Colouring books count as regular content, so use KDP's free ISBN.
- **Adult content:** No
- **AI-generated content:** Yes, for the images. The designs were drawn by a program that an AI assistant wrote. You're required to disclose this; buyers don't see it and it doesn't affect ranking.
- **Proof:** order 1 author copy of each book and check the lines on paper before you publish.
- **Profit:** royalty is 60% of the list price minus printing, which costs roughly $2.50 to $3.00 per book. KDP's pricing page shows the exact figure.
