# Pinterest plan: Mandala series

**Status:** the pins are made (`promo/out/pins/`) but **not scheduled yet**, because Pinterest isn't connected to the
Metricool brand *nexusmedia*. Once you've done the setup below, ask Claude to schedule them. Everything Claude needs is in this file.

## Setup (you)
1. **Get a Pinterest business account** (free). Convert your existing account or make one at business.pinterest.com.
2. **Connect it in Metricool:** Settings › Connections › Pinterest, on the *nexusmedia* brand.
3. **Create 4 boards** with exactly these names:
   - `Mandala Coloring Pages`
   - `Adult Coloring Books`
   - `Coloring Palette Ideas`
   - `Coloring Book Gift Ideas`
4. **Send Claude the 4 Amazon links** once the books are live. Each pin links straight to its book's Amazon page, and that link is where Pinterest beats TikTok.

## Why Pinterest works differently from TikTok
A TikTok post is like a firework: a big bang for 48 hours, then it's gone. A pin is more like a signpost by a busy road.
Pinterest is a search engine, so a pin keeps sending people to Amazon for months. That's why the titles and descriptions
are written as search phrases ("mandala coloring pages for adults") rather than hooks. They use US spelling because most Pinterest searches are American.

## Schedule
One pin a day at most, at **12:00 Sydney time**, which is 8pm US Eastern, Pinterest's busiest hour. Each book's 4 pins
start on its TikTok launch day and run every other day. The gift guide goes out as soon as all four books are live,
because gift pins need a couple of weeks to get going before Christmas.
