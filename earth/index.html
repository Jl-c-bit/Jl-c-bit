How "live" is this?

Geostationary satellites (GOES-East, GOES-West, Himawari) photograph a whole hemisphere every 10 minutes at about 1 km per pixel, so you see weather move in near real time, about 20–40 minutes behind.

Polar-orbiting satellites (VIIRS, MODIS) are sharper (about 250 m) but pass over each spot once or twice a day.

The street-level detail you see when you zoom right in is archived aerial and satellite imagery, the same kind Google Maps uses. Nobody offers a public live feed at that resolution.
