# Brand launch: names and first-week posts

**Chosen names: ListingRadar (Telegram alert bot) and OrderTap (WhatsApp ordering).** The other options below are kept for reference.

All posts go on **new brand accounts only**, never personal ones. Check that each name is free as a handle on Telegram, X, TikTok and Instagram, and as a .com or .app domain, before committing to it.

## Brand names

### Telegram alert bot

| Name | Handle idea | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **ListingRadar** ⭐ | @ListingRadar / @ListingRadarBot | Says exactly what it does: spots new listings first |
| CoinPing | @CoinPingAlerts | Short, sounds like a notification |
| FirstListed | @FirstListedBot | Speaks to wanting to be early |
| PumpWatch | @PumpWatchBot | Grabs attention, but sounds like a pump-and-dump group. Use with care |

Avoid putting "MEXC" in the brand name, because it's their trademark. Saying "alerts for MEXC listings" in the description is fine.

### WhatsApp ordering service

| Name | Why |
|---|---|
| **OrderTap** ⭐ | Short and friendly, works in any country |
| TextMyOrder | Owners instantly get what it does |
| ChatMenu | Describes the experience: the menu lives in the chat |
| NoCommish | Leads with the main selling point: no delivery-app commission |
