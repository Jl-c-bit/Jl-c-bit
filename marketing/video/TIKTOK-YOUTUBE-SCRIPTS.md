# TikTok + YouTube scripts

Every video is a **phone screen recording + voiceover**. No face needed.
Film once, post the same vertical clip to **TikTok** and **YouTube Shorts**.
Then once a week, post one longer video to YouTube (script 9).

How a short works, like a joke: the **hook** (first 2 seconds) is the setup that
stops the scroll, the **demo** is the story, and the **link** is the punchline.
If the hook is weak, nobody hears the rest.

- PrivatePDF: https://chimerical-crostata-fb89ec.netlify.app/pdf/

your **bio** and say "link in bio". On YouTube, pin the link as the first comment and
add it to the description.

## GodMode indicator (money product)

Never say "guaranteed", "easy money" or "you'll be profitable". Both platforms take
down financial-promise content, and it's misleading. Show the tool, not riches.

### 1. "Stop using 7 indicators"  (20s)
- **Hook (on screen text + voice):** "You don't need 7 indicators on your chart."
- **Show:** a messy chart with RSI, MFI, Stoch, CCI all stacked. Swipe → clean
  chart with only GodMode.
- **Voice:** "This one combines RSI, MFI, Stochastic, CCI, Williams, TSI and trend
  into a single 0 to 100 line. When most of them agree, it flags it."
- **End:** "Works on free TradingView. Link in bio."
- **Caption:** One line instead of seven. #tradingview #trading #daytrading #crypto #stocks

### 2. "What happened the last 5 times it lit up"  (30s)
- **Hook:** "I checked the last 5 times this signal fired on Bitcoin."
- **Show:** scroll back on BTC daily, pause on each signal. Be honest: show
  the ones that didn't work too.
- **Voice:** "3 good, 2 not. No indicator is perfect. That's why I use it for
  confluence, not as a buy button."
- **End:** "Script's in my bio if you want to test it yourself."
- **Why:** honesty gets comments ("which 2 failed?") and comments push reach.

### 3. "Install it in 30 seconds"  (30s)
- **Hook:** "How to add a custom indicator to TradingView. Free account works."
- **Show:** Pine Editor → paste → Add to chart → Create Alert.
- **End:** "This is the one I use. Link in bio."

### 4. "Set phone alerts so you stop staring at charts"  (20s)
- **Hook:** "Stop staring at charts all day."
- **Show:** Create Alert → GodMode signal → phone notification popping up.
- **End:** "Get pinged only when 6 of 7 signals agree. Link in bio."

## Setup (10 minutes)
1. TikTok: switch to a **Business account** (Settings → Account), bio:
   *"Tools I build: trading indicator, private PDF, habit app 👇"* + link.
2. YouTube: create a channel, same bio, add the links.
3. Free editing app: CapCut. Use auto-captions; most people watch muted.
4. Metricool is already connected to TikTok, so Claude can schedule uploads once
   videos are saved somewhere it can reach (for example Google Drive).
