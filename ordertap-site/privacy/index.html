OrderTap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

OrderTap provides WhatsApp ordering for local shops and restaurants in Australia (each a "business"). When you message a business's WhatsApp ordering number, OrderTap's automated assistant shows you the menu, takes your order and passes it to that business. This policy explains what information that involves and how we handle it, consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, which WhatsApp provides when you message the business.
  • The messages you send to the ordering number, such as item choices and replies.
  • Order details: the name you give for the order, whether it is pickup or delivery, your delivery address if you choose delivery, the items, prices and the time of the order.

We do not collect payment card details. You pay the business directly. We do not use your WhatsApp profile photo or contacts.

How we use it

  • To show you the menu, build your order and confirm it with you.
  • To send your order to the business so they can prepare and deliver it or hand it over.
  • To let the business see its order history and contact you about your order.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or send you marketing messages.

Who we share it with

  • The business you ordered from, which receives your order details and phone number. The business handles your information under its own privacy practices.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp), which carries the messages between you and the ordering number. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the servers that process and store orders.

Our hosting provider and Meta may store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. We use providers that protect information with industry-standard security. We will also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • An unfinished order (your cart) is held for up to 2 hours, then discarded.
  • Placed orders are kept so the business can see its order history. They are deleted within 30 days after the business stops using OrderTap, or sooner if you ask us (see below).

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We check that incoming messages genuinely come from WhatsApp, and each business's order page is protected by a private key. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email CONTACT_EMAIL with the phone number you ordered from and the business name. We will respond within 30 days. Deleting your information from OrderTap does not delete copies the business or WhatsApp may hold. Contact them directly for those.

Questions and complaints

Contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Children

OrderTap is intended for people who can place orders with local businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
