    How small?

    Compressing turns each page into an image, so text in the new file can't be selected or searched. Best for scans and photo-heavy PDFs.

    How it works

    1. Choose a PDF.
    2. Pick how small you want it.
    3. Tap Compress and download the smaller file.

    Questions

    Why is my compressed file not smaller?

    PDFs that are mostly plain text are already small. Compression helps most with scans and PDFs full of photos. If the result is bigger, keep your original.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. PrivatePDF works entirely inside your browser. Your files never leave your device, and it keeps working even if you go offline after the page loads.
