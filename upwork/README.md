# Upwork starter kit

Everything needed to start earning on Upwork, built from the projects in this repo.
Copy and paste each section into Upwork. Live demos are on
https://chimerical-crostata-fb89ec.netlify.app

Think of Upwork like a farmers' market: the **profile** is your stall sign, the
**portfolio** is the produce on the table, **Project Catalog** gigs are pre-packed
bags people grab without talking to you, and **proposals** are you calling out to
people walking past. You need all four; proposals bring the first sales, the rest
compound.

## 4. Proposal template

Clients read the first two lines in the list view. Make them about **their** job.

```
Hi {name}, you need {restate their goal in one line}. I've built this before:
{most relevant portfolio link}.

How I'd do it:
1. {step}
2. {step}
3. {step}

Timeline: {X} days. Price: ${Y} fixed, or split into {N} milestones.

One question before I start: {a specific question about their job}.

Joshua
```

Rules that win jobs:
- Apply within the first hour of a job being posted (turn on job alerts in the app).
- Target jobs with fewer than 10 proposals, a verified payment method, and a
  client who has hired before.
- Always ask one specific question; it shows you read the post and starts a chat.
- Never work or take payment outside Upwork. It breaks the Terms of Service and
  removes your payment protection.

**Searches to save** (Find Work → Saved searches): `stripe integration`,
`stripe checkout`, `whatsapp bot`, `telegram bot`, `pine script`,
`tradingview indicator`, `payment gateway`, `node.js api`.
